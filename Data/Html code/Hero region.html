About EasyStay

EasyStay is a student-focused platform that helps students moving to new cities find accommodation, room-sharing options, furniture, cooking items, and free resources in one place. It reduces the stress of relocation by offering affordable,simple, and sustainable solutions, allowing students to settle quickly and focus on their studies.
